Currency, medicine, obsession, and revolution — the foods and drinks that shaped human history are stranger than you think.

The Factology Guru Food and Drink Series digs into the origin, science, and cultural history of the world's most iconic foods and drinks. Each book delivers 100 verified facts drawn from culinary history, agricultural records, trade routes, and food science research. Not recipes. Not opinion. The documented truth about the things on your plate and in your glass that changed the world. New titles added regularly.
