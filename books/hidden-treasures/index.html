Frequently Asked Questions About Hidden Treasures

What is Hidden Treasures?

Hidden Treasures is a brand known for bath and body products, like bath bombs and candles, that include a surprise ring inside.

How do I find my ring?

You find your ring by using the product, such as dissolving a bath bomb or burning a candle. The ring is typically enclosed in a protective bag inside.

Can I choose my ring size?

No, you cannot choose your ring size when purchasing Hidden Treasures products. The rings are randomly inserted, adding to the surprise element.

Why are the rings valuable?

The rings vary in value, from costume jewelry to those appraised at up to $10,000. Each ring comes with a code to reveal its potential value online.

Where can I buy Hidden Treasures products?

You can typically buy Hidden Treasures products online through their official website or from various retailers that carry bath and body or novelty items.

What types of products does Hidden Treasures offer?

Hidden Treasures primarily offers bath bombs, candles, and other bath and body items. Each product contains a hidden ring as a surprise element.

How do I appraise my ring?

To appraise your ring, locate the unique code provided with it. Enter this code on the Hidden Treasures website to reveal its estimated value.

Can children use Hidden Treasures bath bombs?

Yes, children can use Hidden Treasures bath bombs, but adult supervision is recommended, especially when retrieving the ring to ensure safety.

Why do some rings have higher value?

Some rings have higher value because they are randomly selected to be part of a special collection or contain more precious metals and gemstones, enhancing the excitement.

What is the return policy for Hidden Treasures products?

The return policy typically applies to unopened, unused products. Once the product is used and the ring revealed, returns are usually not accepted unless defective.
