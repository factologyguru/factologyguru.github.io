Frequently Asked Questions About Oak Island

What is Oak Island?

Oak Island is a small, privately owned island in Lunenburg County, Nova Scotia, Canada, famous for its legend of buried treasure and the ongoing treasure hunt.

Why is Oak Island famous?

Oak Island is famous for the Money Pit, a legendary deep shaft said to contain treasure, booby traps, and other artifacts, attracting treasure hunters for over two centuries.

Who are the current main searchers on Oak Island?

The current main searchers are brothers Rick and Marty Lagina, who purchased a majority stake in the island's treasure-hunting company, Oak Island Tours Inc., in 2006.

How long has the search for treasure on Oak Island been ongoing?

The search has been ongoing for over 220 years, starting with the discovery of the Money Pit in 1795 by Daniel McGinnis, John Smith, and Anthony Vaughan.

What is the Money Pit?

The Money Pit is the legendary site on Oak Island where initial discoverers found a shaft with layers of logs, clay, and coconut fiber, leading to speculation of buried treasure.

Can visitors access Oak Island?

Yes, visitors can access Oak Island. Oak Island Tours offers seasonal guided tours and has a visitor's center, allowing the public to learn about the island's history and mystery.

Where is Oak Island located?

Oak Island is located on the south shore of Nova Scotia, Canada, specifically in Mahone Bay, Lunenburg County, about 60 kilometers southwest of Halifax.

How many people have died during the search for treasure on Oak Island?

At least six people have died over the centuries during various treasure recovery attempts on Oak Island, mostly due to accidents or collapsing shafts.

What kind of treasure is believed to be hidden on Oak Island?

Many theories suggest pirate treasure, Marie Antoinette's jewels, Shakespearean manuscripts, or religious artifacts like the Ark of the Covenant or Holy Grail.

Why is it called the "Curse of Oak Island"?

It's called the "Curse of Oak Island" due to a local legend stating seven men must die before the treasure is found. Six men have already perished.
