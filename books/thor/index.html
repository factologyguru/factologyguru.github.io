Frequently Asked Questions About Thor

What is Thor's primary role in Norse mythology?

Thor is primarily the god of thunder, lightning, storms, and strength. He is a protector of Asgard and Midgard against giants and other threats.

Who are Thor's parents in Norse mythology?

Thor's father is Odin, the All-Father, and his mother is Jörð, the personification of the Earth. This lineage grants him immense power.

What is the name of Thor's famous hammer?

Thor's famous hammer is Mjolnir. It is a powerful magical weapon forged by the dwarves, capable of returning to his hand when thrown.

Where does Thor reside in the Norse cosmos?

Thor resides in Asgard, the realm of the Aesir gods. His magnificent hall is called Bilskirnir, located in the region of Thrudheim, offering sanctuary.

How does Thor travel across the realms?

Thor primarily travels in a chariot pulled by two goats, Tanngrisnir and Tanngnjóstr. He can also use Mjolnir to fly across realms.

Can Thor be defeated in battle according to myths?

Yes, Thor faces numerous challenges and even death. In Ragnarök, he slays Jörmungandr but succumbs to its venom, dying shortly after the battle.

Why is Thor considered a protector deity?

Thor is considered a protector deity due to his unwavering defense of Asgard and Midgard. He constantly battles giants and monstrous beings to maintain cosmic order.

What are some of Thor's key characteristics?

Thor is known for his immense strength, fierce courage, and sometimes hot temper. He is typically depicted as a red-bearded, hammer-wielding, powerful figure.

Who is Thor's wife in Norse mythology?

Thor's wife is Sif, a goddess known for her golden hair. They have a daughter named Thrud. Thor also has sons Magni and Modi with the giantess Járnsaxa.

How was Thor worshipped in ancient times?

Thor was worshipped through sacrifices, prayers, and rituals, particularly by farmers and warriors seeking protection and fertility. His day, Thursday, was considered sacred.
