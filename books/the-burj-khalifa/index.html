Frequently Asked Questions About The Burj Khalifa

What is the Burj Khalifa?

The Burj Khalifa is the world's tallest building, a skyscraper in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. It's a mixed-use development featuring residences, hotels, and observation decks.

Where is the Burj Khalifa located?

The Burj Khalifa is located in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, specifically in the Downtown Dubai area.

How tall is the Burj Khalifa?

The Burj Khalifa stands at an impressive height of 828 meters (2,717 feet) to its tip. This makes it the tallest man-made structure globally.

How long did it take to build the Burj Khalifa?

Construction of the Burj Khalifa began in January 2004 and was completed in 2010. It took approximately six years to build this iconic skyscraper.

What records does the Burj Khalifa hold?

The Burj Khalifa holds numerous world records, including tallest building, highest outdoor observation deck, highest number of stories, and highest occupied floor.

Can visitors go inside the Burj Khalifa?

Yes, visitors can access the Burj Khalifa. There are observation decks "At the Top" on levels 124, 125, and "At the Top SKY" on level 148, offering stunning views.

Why was the Burj Khalifa built?

The Burj Khalifa was built as part of Dubai's strategy to diversify from an oil-based economy to a service and tourism-oriented one. It aimed to put Dubai on the world map.

How many floors does the Burj Khalifa have?

The Burj Khalifa has over 160 stories, though the exact number of habitable floors is 163. This makes it the building with the highest number of floors in the world.

What kind of architecture does the Burj Khalifa feature?

The Burj Khalifa features a Y-shaped tripartite floor geometry inspired by the Hymenocallis flower. This design optimizes residential and hotel space and provides structural stability against wind forces.

Can you stay at the Burj Khalifa?

Yes, you can stay at the Burj Khalifa. It houses the Armani Hotel Dubai, occupying floors from the concourse to level 39, offering luxury accommodation and services.
