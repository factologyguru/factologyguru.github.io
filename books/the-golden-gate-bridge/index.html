Frequently Asked Questions About The Golden Gate Bridge

What is the Golden Gate Bridge?

The Golden Gate Bridge is a suspension bridge spanning the Golden Gate strait, connecting San Francisco Bay to the Pacific Ocean. It's an iconic global landmark known for its distinctive orange color and impressive engineering.

Where is the Golden Gate Bridge located?

The Golden Gate Bridge is located in California, USA, connecting the city of San Francisco on the north end of the San Francisco Peninsula to Marin County.

Why is the Golden Gate Bridge famous?

It's famous for its stunning Art Deco design, vibrant International Orange color, and being a significant engineering marvel of its time. It offers breathtaking views and is a symbol of San Francisco.

How long is the Golden Gate Bridge?

The total length of the Golden Gate Bridge, including approaches, is 1.7 miles (2.7 kilometers). Its main suspension span is 4,200 feet (1,280 meters) long.

What color is the Golden Gate Bridge?

The Golden Gate Bridge is painted "International Orange." This color was chosen for its visibility in fog and its aesthetic appeal against the natural surroundings.

When was the Golden Gate Bridge completed?

The Golden Gate Bridge was completed and opened to vehicular traffic on May 27, 1937. Construction began in January 1933, taking over four years to build.

Who designed the Golden Gate Bridge?

The chief engineer who oversaw the design and construction was Joseph Strauss. Irving Morrow was responsible for the architectural design and the bridge's iconic color choice.

Can you walk or bike across the Golden Gate Bridge?

Yes, you can walk or bike across the Golden Gate Bridge. There are dedicated sidewalks for pedestrians and cyclists, offering stunning views of San Francisco Bay and the Pacific Ocean.

How much did the Golden Gate Bridge cost to build?

The Golden Gate Bridge cost approximately $35 million to build in 1930s dollars. This amount, funded by bonds, is equivalent to several hundred million dollars today, showcasing its monumental investment.

Why is it called the "Golden Gate" Bridge?

It's named after the "Golden Gate Strait," the narrow entrance from the Pacific Ocean into San Francisco Bay, which the bridge spans. The strait itself was named by explorer John C. Frémont in 1846.
